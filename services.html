What we do

Ready-mix concrete, delivery and placement.

Specification-based concrete supplied by the cubic metre, delivered by transit mixer and supported with documentation and placement services.

Products & services

Everything you need to get concrete on site, right.

Our offering is structured around your specified concrete requirement. Final mix design and grade are confirmed against the engineer's specification before production.

Ready-mix by the cubic metre

Concrete supplied per m³ against your approved mix specification, produced under controlled batching.

Multiple strength grades

Different strength classes and grades as required by the project design and approved mix designs.

Scheduled site delivery

Delivery to construction sites using transit mixer trucks, planned to your required date and time.

Repeat-pour programmes

Continuous or phased supply for projects that pour over multiple stages or days.

Site coordination

We plan delivery sequence, access, placement readiness and estimated pour duration with your team.

Records & documentation

Batch and delivery information, plus test records where the project requires them.

Delivery & placement

Pumping and placement, where it makes sense.

For sites with difficult access or high placement points, we arrange concrete pumping and special placement, either through owned equipment or approved third-party providers, when it is commercially and technically appropriate.

  • Transit-mixer deliveryReliable dispatch coordinated to your pour sequence.
  • Access & readiness planningWe confirm site access and placement readiness before dispatch.
  • Out-of-hours & urgent supplyAvailable where operating and logistics conditions allow.
