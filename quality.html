Quality & technical control

Quality is an operating requirement, not an afterthought.

We maintain documented procedures across the full production cycle, so concrete performance is controlled and traceable from material acceptance to delivery.

Where a project requires it, we keep traceability from the approved mix design through batching and delivery, with test and delivery records retained for project documentation.

Our quality system covers
  • Raw-material acceptanceInspection and control of aggregates, cement, water and admixtures before use.
  • Batching & calibrationBatching to approved mix designs with calibrated dosing systems.
  • Sampling & testingTesting arrangements for concrete cubes, aggregates and materials.
  • Non-conformance controlDocumented handling of any material or product that does not conform.
  • Record keepingBatch and delivery records retained for project documentation.
  • TraceabilityFrom approved mix design through batching to delivery, where specified.
Standards alignment

Aligned to Ghanaian concrete standards.

The Ghana Standards Authority lists GS 1317, covering general requirements for the competency of ready-mixed concrete production establishments, among Ghana's civil-construction standards. GSA also identifies testing of concrete cubes, aggregates and other construction materials within its laboratory activities.

We align our quality system with applicable Ghanaian standards and will obtain the relevant approvals, certifications and testing arrangements before commercial scale-up.
