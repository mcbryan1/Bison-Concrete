How it works

A controlled path from order to pour.

Enquiry & requirement review

We confirm grade or mix specification, volume, delivery location and the date and time you need concrete on site.

Quotation & order confirmation

A commercial quotation based on the approved specification, quantity, haulage distance and any additional services, then production scheduling.

Batching & dispatch

Material inspection and batching to the approved mix design, loading into transit mixers and dispatch to the delivery plan.

Delivery, discharge & records

Site delivery and discharge, completion of delivery documentation, and quality records kept per the agreed programme.

Quality & standards

Traceable from mix design to delivery.

We align our quality system with applicable Ghanaian standards and maintain documented procedures for material acceptance, batching, calibration, sampling, testing and record keeping.

The Ghana Standards Authority lists GS 1317 for ready-mixed concrete production establishments. We build toward the relevant approvals, certifications and testing arrangements before commercial scale-up.
