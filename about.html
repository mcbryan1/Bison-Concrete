Who we are

Concrete built on consistency, delivery and discipline.

A ready-mix concrete business established to serve the Greater Accra construction corridor with specification-based supply and dependable logistics.

A professional ready-mix operation, from Tema Community 18.

Bison Concrete Ltd was established to supply consistent, specification-based concrete to residential, commercial, industrial and civil construction across Tema, Accra and surrounding areas.

We combine controlled batching, quality assurance, professional dispatch and reliable delivery, so contractors and developers receive concrete produced to an agreed mix and delivered inside the required placement window. The model is scalable: additional mixers, pumping capability and production capacity come on as order volumes grow.

Vision & mission

Trusted concrete, delivered with discipline.

Our vision

To become a trusted ready-mix concrete supplier in Ghana, recognised for consistent quality, reliable delivery and professional project support.

Our mission
