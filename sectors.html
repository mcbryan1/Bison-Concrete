How we plan service coverage.

Tema and the wider Greater Accra corridor let us serve residential developments, commercial buildings, warehouses, industrial facilities, roads and drainage works from one base.

Actual coverage for any order is set by haulage time, traffic, order size and the required placement schedule, so concrete arrives fresh and within its placement window.
